# HOMFLY-PT Tails

Visualizations of the HOMFLY-PT Tails.

To visualize the tail phenomenon of the colored HOMFLY-PT polynomials, we normalize each polynomial by its lowest q and highest a power for the tail, and vice versa for the head. 

* x axis: q powers
* y axis: a powers
* heatmap: coefficient of monomials, where orange is +1, green is -1, red is > 1, and blue is < 1
* timestep: the color of the polynomial
